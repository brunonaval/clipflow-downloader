# ClipFlow Downloader — Legal Usage Policy

This document describes the permitted and prohibited uses of ClipFlow Downloader.

## Permitted Uses

ClipFlow Downloader may be used to download media content in the following situations:

- **Your own content**: videos, audio, or files you created and uploaded yourself.
- **Authorized content**: content for which you have explicit written or contractual permission from the rights holder.
- **Public domain**: works where copyright has expired or was never applicable, in the relevant jurisdiction.
- **Creative Commons**: content published under a Creative Commons license that permits downloading and/or redistribution (e.g., CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0). Always verify the specific license terms.
- **Platform-permitted downloads**: content where the platform explicitly allows downloading through its own features or terms of service (e.g., a podcast feed, a platform's official offline feature, a site that explicitly grants download rights).

## Prohibited Uses

ClipFlow Downloader must not be used to:

- **Bypass DRM**: circumvent Digital Rights Management systems, encryption, or technical protection measures applied to any content.
- **Bypass paywalls**: access or download content that requires a paid subscription or purchase without completing that payment.
- **Unauthorized downloading**: download any copyrighted content without permission from the rights holder.
- **Circumvent authentication or access controls**: use the tool to access content that requires login, regional restriction bypass, or any other access control you are not authorized to bypass.
- **Credential or cookie harvesting**: collect, store, or transmit login credentials, session tokens, or cookies in any unauthorized manner.
- **Violate platform Terms of Service**: use the tool in ways that violate the terms of service of any platform, even if technically possible.
